05 • COMPARE

PCA vs t-SNE

Both reduce dimensions, but they answer different questions.

AspectPCAt-SNE
TypeLinearNon-linear
Main ideaMaximizes variancePreserves local similarities
SpeedUsually fasterUsually slower
InterpretabilityComponents have mathematical meaningAxes are mainly visual
Best useReduction + visualizationCluster visualization
Quick takeaway: PCA is useful when you want an interpretable, variance-based transformation. t-SNE is especially useful when you want to visually inspect local clusters.
